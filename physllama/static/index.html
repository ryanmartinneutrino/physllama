A quick start

  1. Connect your sensor

    Turn on a supported Bluetooth device. Search, then connect one of the recognized devices. Close other apps connected to it. Match the printed device ID to the scan result before connecting. You can rename a device while stopped; its hardware identity stays visible.

  2. Choose what to measure

    Use each graph’s Sensors dropdown to choose channels, then set the sampling interval in milliseconds. Sensors checked on any graph are recorded once when you press Start. Smaller intervals collect more often.

  3. Make it visible

    Start collection. Use each graph’s “Sensors” dropdown to overlay channels from one or more devices. Stop collection before adding new sensors to record. Different units get separate labeled axes. Add graphs to compare views.

  4. Explore and keep your data

    Stop, zoom, and hover for values. Each graph’s Download CSV button exports all samples for that graph’s selected sensors, including samples outside the zoomed view. Export CSV at the top exports the series shown across all graphs.

Units come from the device library. Smart Cart position starts at zero each run; its velocity and speed are derived from the last three position samples. Go Direct uses the requested interval for timing; PASCO uses actual polling times, so its rate may be lower than requested. Devices are not hardware synchronized. Each Start creates a new run.

Data lives in server memory. Refreshing this page keeps it; stopping the app or resetting clears it. All browsers connected to this service share acquisition controls and data. Export before closing the app. Demo data is clearly labeled and cannot be mixed with real measurements.

Clear this experiment’s data?

All recorded runs will be removed. Your devices and graph layout will stay connected. Export first if you want to keep the measurements.

Rename device

Leave blank to restore the advertised name. This name is saved on the computer running PhysLlama and shared by its connected browsers. The device’s IDs stay visible.

Quit PhysLlama?

Export your data first. Quitting stops collection, disconnects your devices, and clears this session’s measurements.
