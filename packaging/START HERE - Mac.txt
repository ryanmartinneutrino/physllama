VERNIER LAB — MAC INSTALLATION

1. Choose the right download
   Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip" means Apple Silicon (arm64).
   "Processor: Intel" means Intel (x64). macOS 13 or later is required.

2. Install
   Extract the ZIP. In Finder, open your home folder and create an
   Applications folder if needed. Drag Vernier Lab.app into that folder.
   No Terminal commands, Python installation, or internet are needed.

   Optional: double-click Install Vernier Lab.command and answer y to copy
   the app automatically. macOS may require a separate Open Anyway approval
   for this script; dragging the app avoids that extra step.

3. First launch: Open Anyway
   Double-click the installed Vernier Lab.app. This download is unsigned
   and unnotarized, so macOS may say its developer cannot be verified.
   After trying to open it, dismiss that warning, then:

   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down to Security.
   - Click Open Anyway for Vernier Lab.
   - Confirm Open; enter your password or use Touch ID if requested.

   Approve only the app you intended to download. You do not need to turn
   off macOS security globally. Future launches normally need no approval.
   Apple guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

4. Bluetooth
   Allow Bluetooth when prompted. If access was denied, check System
   Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth, enable the app shown there,
   then quit and reopen Vernier Lab. Turn on your sensor, keep it nearby,
   close other sensor apps, and choose Search for devices, then Connect.
   Connection progress appears in the browser; transient failures trigger
   up to three connection attempts. After failure, wake the sensor and search again.

5. Finish or update
   Download CSV before quitting. Use Quit app in the browser header to
   stop the service and release Bluetooth. Closing the tab keeps it running.
   Before updating, quit the app and move the old app to Trash, then copy
   the new app into place. Your recorded data is lost when the app quits.

Diagnostics: ~/Library/Logs/Vernier Lab/app.log
